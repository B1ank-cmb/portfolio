A ceramicist's portfolio. The opposite discipline from the others: the work is the loudest thing on the page and the interface stays quiet. Masonry grid, keyboard-accessible lightbox, no decoration competing with the pieces.

How I work

The owner comes first

If the staff can't update the site themselves, I built the wrong thing. Every project ships with a plain-language guide written for the person who'll actually use it.

Fast by default

Static where possible, JavaScript only where it earns its place. Most of these pages ship less than 30KB and load instantly on a phone with two bars.

Clean handoff

You get the repo, the docs, and an explanation of every decision. The goal is that you don't need me after launch — and call me back because you want to, not because you're stuck.

Honest scoping

I'll tell you when something you're asking for is a bad idea, and when a project is bigger than your budget. Both conversations are cheaper before the work than after.

Got a project?

Tell me what the business does and what's broken. I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person for it.
