    Good chicken, good people, good day

    We're a small crew with a big love for good food. Every sandwich is breaded by hand to order — no heat lamps, no shortcuts, no sitting around waiting for a customer.

    The menu rotates every few weeks because we get bored, and because Miami deserves better than the same sandwich forever. Some things stay (the Classic isn't going anywhere). Everything else is fair game.

    Thanks for supporting our dream and letting us do what we love every day.

    — The Bird & Bun Crew
