Why these cost what they cost

Real thread, not glue

Glued spines crack and shed pages within a year. Ours are sewn in signatures, so the book opens flat and stays together after it's been in a bag for two years.

Paper that takes ink

120gsm with a hard size. Fountain pens don't feather, markers don't bleed to the next page, and you can write on both sides. Most notebooks at this price use 70–80gsm.

Small runs

Two hundred at a time. It's more expensive per unit and it means we sell out, but it also means we can fix something in the next run instead of sitting on ten thousand of a mistake.

Use it up guarantee

If the binding fails while there are still blank pages, we replace it. No receipt, no argument, no time limit.
